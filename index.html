Arena shooter

FUNNEL

Have fun in the tunnel

Scoring

First to 1000 points wins. Each enemy kill is +1. Every second an ally stands in the enemy home zone is +1 per fighter.

Push into enemy territory. Hold the line. First team to one thousand points owns the tunnel.

Warning

Contains flashing lights and rapid visual effects. Stop playing if you feel unwell or uncomfortable.

Controls

Move
WASD, Shift sprint, Space jump
Stance
hold C crouch
Weapon
1-0 select, LMB primary, RMB alt-fire, V or MMB aim (FPS)
Pointer
Click the arena or press P
Team
T dev flip (hire prep)
Dev
K kill self — auto-respawn after 5 s
